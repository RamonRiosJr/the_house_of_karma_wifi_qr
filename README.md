## 📱 Troubleshooting & Manual Connect (For Guests)

A prominent fallback block is built into the bottom of the card for guests with older phone cameras, laptops, or weak camera focus:

* **Giant Monospace Password**: `KarmaTea2026!`
* **1-Click Copy**: Copies network name or password to clipboard with a visual checkmark toast.
* **Show/Hide Toggle**: Privacy toggle (`👁️` / `🙈`) to mask or reveal the password on screen.
* **Step-by-Step Instructions**:
  1. Open device Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Select `KarmaHGuest`.
  3. Enter `KarmaTea2026!`.

## 🖨️ Printing Instructions

1. Open [index.html](file:///c:/Users/localadmin/OneDrive%20-%20Coqui%20Cloud/Documents/GitHub/the_house_of_karma_wifi_qr/index.html) in your browser.
2. Select your desired theme or click **Crisp Print** for maximum toner economy.
3. Click **Print Sign** (or press `Ctrl + P`).
4. All toolbar buttons, toggles, and screen controls automatically disappear, leaving a clean, perfectly centered tabletop card.
